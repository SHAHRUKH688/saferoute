Say this upfront
  • The rail network is a simplified schematic of major junctions. Distances are straight-line distance × 1.22, and speeds are class averages. They are not timetable data.
  • Risk scores are synthetic: a seeded base value per track, multiplied by region and season. The router accepts any risk source, which is the point.
  • The certificate lists no syllabus, so say "DSA concepts applied", not "the course covered X".
  • Keep any safety motivation general. Do not describe an unfiled invention in a public talk.
  • If asked how it was built: you designed the problem and the model, built it with AI assistance, and verified it with the test suite. Be ready to walk through the five functions in the table.
